# Copy Variants

Read the profile's VOICE CASCADE first (project.md + voices/vc1.md + relevant
channel guidelines) — variants must stay on-voice and respect per-channel rules.

## Modes
1. **Cross-channel adaptation**: take a source post/brief and re-express it
   per target channel/platform. Not truncation — re-think the native format
   (X = punchy thread w/ standalone first tweet; LinkedIn = story+insight,
   line breaks; IG caption = hook line then depth; TikTok = spoken-word script).
   Honor each target channel's guidelines. For video/carousel/multi-slide: don't
   stop at the hook — plan a pattern interrupt or beat change every few
   seconds/slides (new visual, new claim, re-hook mid-way). Platform feeds rank
   on completion/watch-through rate more than the opening line alone (TikTok
   Creator Portal, YouTube Creator Academy audience-retention guidance) — a
   strong hook with a sagging middle still underperforms.
2. **Hook/headline batch**: 5-10 variants on one idea using distinct mechanisms —
   curiosity gap (Loewenstein's information-gap theory), bold claim, question,
   stat, story-open, contrarian, and high-arousal emotion (awe, anger, anxiety,
   amusement — the specific emotions Berger & Milkman's peer-reviewed study
   ["What Makes Online Content Viral?", J. Marketing Research 49(2), 2012] found
   predict sharing; low-arousal states like sadness or contentment do not).
   Label each variant by mechanism, so losing variants teach something.
3. **A/B pairs**: two versions differing on ONE variable, stated.

Rules: every variant respects banned words and CTA rules from the profile voice.
If the source idea is weak, say so before generating ten variants of weak. A
hook earns the click; retention and emotional arousal earn the share — don't
optimize the first line and stop there.
