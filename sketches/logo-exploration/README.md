## myOS logo exploration

### Brief
Bold, futuristic, AI-native, but simple. Apple-like icon clarity and friendliness. Abstract operating-system symbol. Must survive app-icon and small navigation sizes.

### Directions

#### Kernel
System layers orbit one personal core. Strongest standalone silhouette and best small-size behavior.

#### Portal
Two loops imply input and output, human and system, thought and action. Most intelligent and premium, but slightly more complex.

#### Pinwheel
Four modules form one continuous operating system. Friendliest and most modular, but risks reading as generic collaboration software.

### Shared choices
- Warm myOS accent: `#B54A2B`
- Neutral ink: `#25231F`
- Native SF Pro wordmark
- Rounded app-icon container with flat, high-contrast geometry
- No literal initials, brain, robot, sparkle, or command prompt

### Recommendation
Advance Kernel. Distinct at 16 px, scalable to monochrome, compatible with current product color system.
